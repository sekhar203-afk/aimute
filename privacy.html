AI Mute – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

Short version

AI Mute collects no personal data. Nothing you see or type leaves your browser.

What the extension stores

On your own device only (Chrome's extension storage):

  • Your on/off switches for each site and option.
  • A count of how many AI items were hidden, per day and per site.
  • The date you installed it (to decide when to show a "leave a review" note).
  • The latest list of hiding rules.

This data is never sent to us or to anyone else. Uninstalling the extension deletes it.

Network requests

Once a day, AI Mute may download a public file of hiding rules (CSS selectors and text patterns). This request sends no personal information, cookies or browsing data. The file is data only and is never run as code.

Website access

AI Mute runs only on Google Search, Gmail, Google Drive, Google Docs, YouTube and Amazon pages. It looks at the page only to find and hide AI features. It does not read, save or send your searches, emails, documents or purchases.

No selling, no ads, no tracking

We do not sell or share data, we show no ads, and we use no analytics.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, we will update this page and tell users inside the extension before the change applies.
